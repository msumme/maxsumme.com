I had an odd dream recently. I was walking through a familiar town, and suddenly was hit with the idea "Your day as art". Suddenly the simple act of walking around had some sort of cosmic significance, as though it were being painted onto the fabric of time for eternity, and I felt more alive and aware of everything around me.

For some reason, this really stuck with me when I woke up, completely exhausted, having slept too long into the morning, and having few plans for the day. The dream felt rather like an accusation.

How often do we think of our day, not the things we accomplish, but the very living and doing, down to the smallest details, such as brushing your teeth, or taking out the trash, as something that can be done beautifully, with care and attention?

I for one, often trudge through unpleasant tasks, grumbling, hoping that all this trudging will bring me at last to something worthwhile, having endured so much.

What if instead, we pour out our very selves into the world, to make it something better than it is, whether it be by doing our work well or simply showing a special kindness to someone, making something beautiful?

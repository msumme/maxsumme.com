# If your old software crashed today, how would it impact your business?

### Without a tested, secure recovery plan, your business could be offline in moments, with no clear solution.

\\ Becoming slower and less agile—even falling behind your competition—is a common and serious reality for many businesses. Adding quick fixes, layers of patch-worked IT processes, and too many "special" manual processes can make you overly reliant on key personnel. You may feel there is no time to upgrade your systems, or not enough of the resources needed to fix the problem. It's overwhelming, and you need a way forward. If this sounds familiar, I have good news!\\

- \\ \\
- I provide IT roadmaps and a systematic approach for gradual upgrades—or, a full replacement of your current systems, depending on your needs and resources.

- The steps I custom develop for you will alleviate some of your worst problems quickly, while providing a clear path towards systems and IT processes that grow with your business.

### 

### Here's how we'll develop your **LEGACY SYTEM ESCAPE PLAN**

\\ Most aging software systems can be replaced with minimal or no down time, given the right approach. That approach should be built around your specific circumstances, systems, and the particular way that *you* do business.\\

- \\ \\
- First, I document and become familiar with your outdated and inefficient IT processes, drafting a way to eliminate the worst offenders first, freeing up more of your time and resources for growing your business.

- Next, we identify key personnel and create a strategy to provide redundancy during the transition, allowing greater flexibility and reducing crises caused by sick days and vacation time.

- Finally, we focus on the future, and the importance of a modern IT infrastructure. With modern power and reliability you will be able to implement those projects and initiatives—essential to the growth and health of your business—that your old systems could not support.

### 

### This is your **LEGACY SYTEM ESCAPE PLAN**

\\ A roadmap includes an analysis of your current situation, recommendations for first steps, and identified areas of special concern, as well as identified future capabilities that will be possible with a stronger foundation. This does not include detailed project planning (sprints, feature sets), but it does include an outline of how you will start to regain control of your IT processes. I will also provide research into your technology stack and offer recommendations about existing solutions that may available to solve particular needs unique to your situation. While not a detailed technical specification, my roadmap clearly outlines the steps needed to fix your ailing IT systems and strategy, and the intermediate action you can take to alleviate the worst pains of your current situation.\\

### 

### An IT roadmap grows your business.

\\ Modern business needs modern IT, not only to stay competitive, but to stay open.  Companies that view IT as an area of investment for growth instead of a necessary cost center, adapt faster to industry change, reach new levels of operational efficiency, and are able to pursue state-of-the-art R&D.\\

- \\ \\
- Operational stability gives you the confidence you need to take on new challenges.

- Key personnel are less worried about their day-to-day, and able to focus on continuing improvements.

- Employees are more energized when they are working for an efficient, modern business, invested in current technology.

- You are poised to adopt state-of-the-art technological advances and to improve your processes continually when your IT can keep up.

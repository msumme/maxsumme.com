In short, my suggestion is: don't.\\ \\ Waking up early isn't the key to a productive and successful life.  It isn't some panacea.  It isn't going to change everything.\\ \\ What will change everything, however, is your relationship with your first moments of your day.\\

## Don't Hit Snooze

\\ The most important part of your day is when your alarm goes off.  Immediately responding to that alarm by getting out of bed will make a powerful difference.  It sets the tone for self-control you have throughout the rest of the day.\\ \\ It is often the most difficult moment of your day.  Your bed is warm and soft.  The room is cold.  You're hungry and tired, and if you're anything like me, you basically HATE mornings.  I never feel worse than in my first moments of the day, especially if I happened to deal with some middle of the night insomnia the night before.\\ \\ But I keep a consistent wake-up time during the week.  Every day, I get up at 6:10am.\\ \\ "Wait, I thought you said don't get up early".\\ \\ That's right - I don't actually want to get up this early.  If I could, I would sleep until 7:30 or 8 every single day.  (That I think of that as sleeping in is a testament to the power of crying babies to change your morning routine).\\ \\ But because I cannot sleep in, and have a reason to be up early, I jump out of bed when the alarm goes off.  It helps that I don't want to torment my wife with this wakeup time she neither needs or wants, but I've noticed that as I've gotten into the habit of jumping up when the alarm goes off, and not getting back into bed, I've started to feel an increasing sense of ownership over my day.\\ \\ This day can't stop me.  Not even when I don't get enough sleep.  Not even when I have a mild cold.\\ \\ And that, in turn, has given me the power to do another thing I find it hard to do: Go to the gym twice a week.\\

## It's Not About When You Wake Up

\\ What's important about your morning is the tone you set for your day.  Is it a tone that your mood is going to dictate your productivity?  Or is it that you're going to make decisions about what you spend your day doing?\\ \\ You can't count on waking up feeling motivated every single day.  But you can count on being disciplined every single day.\\ \\ The alarm clock is your first small hill to climb.  If you climb it, you will have built a little more discipline that day.  And more the next day.\\ \\
